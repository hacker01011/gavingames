Sky Park Candy Run

One loop through every street in Sky Park, starting at Skypark Dr and Airstream Ct. Walkways link the cul-de-sacs, so you never hit a dead end or backtrack. Navigator Drive is the jackpot.

Strats

Go at 5:45

Sunset is about 6:10. Starting a little before means full bowls, and the full-size bars are still there. You finish around 7:45, right when houses start running low.

Navigator first

Navigator Dr has the most houses of any street here, about 60, all close together. Hitting it early gets you the biggest haul before other groups clean it out.

Walkway shortcuts

The end of Airstream Ct has a walkway to the end of Aviator Ct. The end of Piper Cub Ct has one to Coast Range. They're short paths, so bring a flashlight.

Zig-zag or one side?

Rule: if you walk a street twice, do one side each time. If you only walk it once, zig-zag both sides. Coast Range is the only street you walk twice. Keep the houses on your right both times and you never cross it at all. A grown-up decides where it's safe to cross on the zig-zag streets.

Read the porch

Porch light on, decorations up, pumpkins lit: go. Dark house: skip it, don't knock. The houses with huge decorations usually have the best candy.

Green strip paths

The route already uses two green-strip walkways (stops 2 and 4). There's also a trail around the whole edge of the neighborhood, plus a path through the middle from Coast Range to Skypark Dr. They don't add houses, but they make good shortcuts back to the start if you need a bathroom or a bag swap.

Pillowcase + a dump bag

Carry a pillowcase, and have a grown-up hold a second bag. Dump into it after Navigator so a heavy bag never slows you down.

Still going? Bonus round

East of the park, Ponderosa Rd, Oak Circle and Mountain View Dr are packed with houses too. Get there by car, not on foot. Some of those streets may be in a private community, so check first.

Stay safe

Grown-up with you the whole time. Glow sticks or a flashlight. Don't walk along Mount Hermon Rd, it's busy. Candy gets checked at home before anyone eats it.
